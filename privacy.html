Cookies and Similar Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you and enhance your experience on our Services. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences. We use cookies to remember your login information, track your learning progress, and understand how you interact with our platform.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality, security, and authentication. These cookies are necessary for our Services to function properly.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Services by collecting anonymous data about page views, session duration, and bounce rates.
  • Functional Cookies: Enable personalization features such as remembering your preferences and language settings.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track your activity across websites to deliver targeted advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services such as Google Analytics and advertising partners that may set their own cookies on your device. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of cookies. We encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle your information. You can opt out of certain third-party cookies through your browser settings or by visiting the opt-out pages provided by these services.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, blocking cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our Services. You can also use browser extensions to manage cookies more granularly.

For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit www.allaboutcookies.org or your browser's help documentation.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction. Our security practices include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure authentication mechanisms including password hashing and two-factor authentication options
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Limited access to personal information to authorized personnel only
  • Incident response procedures to address potential data breaches promptly

While we strive to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the context:

  • Account Information: Retained for the duration of your account and for a reasonable period afterward for legal compliance

  • Analytics Data: Typically retained for 12-24 months unless aggregated or anonymized
